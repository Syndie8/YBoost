// FAQ

AI visibility for real estate agents: common questions

What is AI visibility for a real estate agent?
AI visibility is how often AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google's AI answers, Perplexity and Claude name you when someone asks about real estate agents in your suburb. A vendor might ask who the best agent in their area is, or who to call for an appraisal. If your name is in the answer, you are on their shortlist before they have looked at a single website.
Can ChatGPT recommend individual real estate agents?
Yes. Ask for the best agent in a suburb and AI assistants often answer with named agents, sometimes alongside their agencies. Which names they choose depends on what they can find about each agent online, such as reviews, portal profiles, agency bios and local news.
How do I get ChatGPT to recommend me as a real estate agent?
There is no switch to flip, but you can make yourself much easier to recommend. Keep your name, suburbs and specialties consistent across your portal profiles, agency bio and Google. Collect recent reviews that mention the suburbs you work. Publish clear, factual information about your results and your area. YBoost shows which of these matter most in your suburb and checks each week whether the changes are working. No one can guarantee a mention, and we do not promise one.
I work for a franchise. Can I still improve my AI visibility?
Yes. Much of what AI can find about an agent is tied to the person rather than the brand: your reviews, your profiles, your bio and what is written about you. You can update most of that yourself, or with a quick request to your office. You do not need head office to change the agency website.
Does my AI visibility stay with me if I change agencies?
Much of it does. Reviews, press and profiles tied to your name travel with you. Some pages belong to the agency, such as your bio on its website, so when you move, update your details everywhere and keep your name and suburbs consistent.

You get the full findings, a comparison with the agents AI names instead of you, citation evidence, prioritised fixes, three ready-to-use content drafts, a 20-minute walkthrough and a re-check at 60 days. The first five customers pay A$299 in exchange for feedback. Recommendations do not guarantee AI mentions, rankings or listings won.
How does monitoring work?
Monitoring is A$99 a month for one agent in one suburb, plus A$39 a month for each extra suburb. Every week YBoost runs a full check, asking the full set of vendor questions across every AI engine we track, and emails you what changed. You get up to 8 checks per suburb every 30 days, and the weekly checks count toward that, so you have a few extra to run yourself after you make a change. You also get a to-do list of fixes, AI drafts you can use, a comparison with up to 20 rival agents, and a vendor-pitch PDF with your logo. You do not need to buy the report and fix pack first.
Is this just SEO with a new name?
They are related but not the same. SEO is about ranking in a list of links. AI visibility is about being named in the answer itself. That depends on clear, consistent facts about you appearing in the sources AI assistants trust. Work you have already done on your online profile usually helps, and nothing YBoost recommends will hurt your Google rankings.
Is this the same as GEO (generative engine optimisation)?
Yes. GEO, short for generative engine optimisation, is the industry name for improving how AI assistants describe and recommend you. YBoost is GEO built for real estate agents in Australia: it checks whether AI names you in your suburb and tells you what to change, in plain English.
Which AI assistants does YBoost check?
YBoost checks ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google's AI answers (AI Overviews and AI Mode) on Monitoring. The free snapshot uses three of them: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity. Each check asks the questions vendors in your area ask, such as who the best agent in a suburb is or who to call for an appraisal before selling.

Monitoring is A$99 a month for one suburb, plus A$39 a month for each extra suburb. The report and fix pack is a one-off A$399, or A$299 for the first five customers. All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST.
Do I need technical skills to act on the recommendations?
No. Every recommendation is written in plain English. If you can update your profile, ask a client for a review or send a change to your office, you can make these changes.
