Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI visibility for real estate agents?
AI visibility is how often AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini mention and recommend your agency when someone asks about real estate agents in your suburb. Vendors are already asking these questions. If your agency is not in the answer, you are not on the shortlist.
Why do AI assistants keep recommending the big franchise brands?
AI models lean on volume: national websites, decades of press coverage and thousands of pages that mention the franchise name. That reflects how much has been written about a brand, not how well it sells homes in your suburb. The gap is fixable, and independents are best placed to fix it, because they control their own website, reviews and public profile.
I own my agency and my website. Why does that matter here?
Because most AI visibility fixes are website and content changes. A franchise agent has to route those through head office and a brand team, which can take months or simply never happen. You can publish them the same day. YBoost is built for owners who can act on a recommendation this week, not next quarter.
Is this just SEO with a new name?
They are related but not the same. SEO is about ranking in a list of links. AI visibility is about being named in the answer itself. That depends on clear, consistent facts about your agency appearing in the sources AI assistants trust, which is a different job to chasing keywords and backlinks. Good news: the work you have already done on SEO usually helps, and nothing YBoost recommends will hurt your Google rankings.
Which AI assistants does YBoost check?
YBoost checks how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity answer the questions vendors in your area actually ask, such as who the best agent in a suburb is, or who to get an appraisal from before selling.
Do I need technical skills to act on the recommendations?
No. Every recommendation is written in plain English. If you can edit your own website, or brief the person who does, you can make these changes. That is exactly why YBoost is built for agents who own their site rather than agents locked into a franchise template.
